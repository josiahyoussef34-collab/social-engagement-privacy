Data deletion instructions

Part of the Privacy Policy · Last updated: 25 October 2026

You can ask for information that the Social Engagement Control Centre holds about you, or about your organisation, to be deleted or corrected. You do not need to have used the software, and you do not need to give a reason.

How to ask

Email Josiah Youssef, the developer and operator of the software, at josiahyoussef34@gmail.com. A subject line such as "Data deletion request" or "Correction request" helps, but any clear request will be handled.

Tell me anything that helps me find the information, for example:

  • your organisation's LinkedIn page, Facebook Page or Instagram account, to delete what is held about that account;
  • a link to a particular post; or
  • your Instagram username, to delete your comments and username (the words of a comment help me find it).

If you are an organisation, tell me whether you also want me to stop reading your accounts in future. I will then remove them from the list the software reads.

For a correction, tell me what is wrong and what it should say. If I do not agree to change it, you can ask me to attach a statement of the correction you wanted.

You can ask for someone else if you have their authority. I will never ask for a password.

What happens next

  1. I acknowledge your request. I ask for more information only if I cannot find the records or need to check that you are the person or organisation concerned (or are authorised to act for them). I ask only for what is needed and use it only to handle your request.
  2. I find the information. The software's deletion tool searches every text record in its database for the name, username or other detail you gave me, including spelling variants with or without macrons. It also reports backups, other database copies and working files in the software's own data and session folders that contain it, and saved images belonging to matching posts. I then check things the tool cannot search, such as screenshots and images (it cannot recognise people in pictures).
  3. I remove it. After I check the tool's findings and confirm, the tool removes the matching text from the database and keeps a record of the request that does not contain the deleted information. Your comments lose their text and username; other mentions of you are replaced with "[redacted]". I then delete relevant saved images and screenshots, and delete the backups and older database copies that contain the information or replace them with a new backup made after the deletion.
  4. I tell you the outcome, including anything I have kept and why.

I aim to complete requests within 20 working days.

What the tool can and cannot do

The deletion tool works on the software's records on the operating computer. It finds text that matches what you gave me; it does not find every possible copy on its own. In particular, I deal by hand with:

  • screenshots and images, which are reviewed by eye;
  • backups and older copies, which the tool lists but does not delete;
  • copies made outside the software's own folders, for example during development or testing, which the tool does not search;
  • records kept by the AI assistant I use, which are reviewed by hand or expire under the assistant's own settings; and
  • copies held outside the computer by service providers (see Information held by others).

What may be kept

Approved action plans, and the posts, suggestions and decisions they are based on, are an audit trail of what was approved and done. The deletion tool does not change them. (Other people's comments are not part of that trail and are always removed.) If your information is in one of those records, I will decide whether that record is still needed. If it is not, I will remove it. If it is, I will keep only what is needed, tell you what and for how long, and not use it for anything else. I may also keep information where the law requires it or to deal with a legal claim. I keep a short record that your request was received and completed; it does not contain the deleted information.

Information held by others

  • Social media platforms. Deleting information from the software does not delete your posts, comments or profile on LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram. If one of the business accounts I manage commented on or liked your post and you want that removed, tell me. I will review it and, if I agree, remove it by hand on the platform.
  • AI providers. Under OpenAI's published terms, information sent for an AI suggestion may stay in its abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Information read by the Claude AI assistant is kept by Anthropic under the account's terms and settings (see the Privacy Policy). I cannot delete these providers' copies myself.

Facebook and Instagram users

Members of the public never sign in to the software, with Facebook, Instagram or otherwise, so it does not appear in your Facebook or Instagram settings and there is nothing to remove there. Please use the email process above. It covers all information the software holds that came from Facebook or Instagram.

If you are not satisfied

Contact me first. You can also complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz. In Australia, you may be able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
